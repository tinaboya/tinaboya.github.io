### About me

I am a doctoral research assistant associate at [Université de Genève](https://www.unige.ch/medecine/radio/groupes-de-recherche/1035teodoro/membres-du-groupe/boya-zhang). Prior to that, I obtained MSc of Electrical Engineering in February 2021 at [Brussels School of Engineering](https://www.bruface.eu/). I obtained BSc of Information Engineering in June 2019 at [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://en.nwpu.edu.cn/). I completed a research internship at the [Center for Law & Economics at ETH Zurich](https://lawecon.ethz.ch/) from June to October 2021.

My research interests are in applied NLP, biomedical NLP, and developing NLP methods in the context of misinformation, internet safety, and the intersection of NLP and healthcare.
