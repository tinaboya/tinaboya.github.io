About

I work on trustworthy machine learning for high-stakes decision making, with healthcare as my main application area. I defended my PhD in Life Sciences (AI for Healthcare) at the Université de Genève in September 2026, in the Data Science for Digital Health group of Prof. Douglas Teodoro. From November 2025 to April 2026 I was a visiting researcher at MIT's Laboratory for Computational Physiology with Prof. Leo A. Celi.

Before my PhD, I obtained an MSc in Electrical Engineering (ir., with Distinction) from Vrije Universiteit Brussel / Université libre de Bruxelles in 2021 and a BEng in Information Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2019, and I was a research intern at the Center for Law & Economics, ETH Zurich in 2021.

Research interests

Trustworthy machine learning for high-stakes decision making:

  • Transparent human–AI interaction: reliable evidence-based decision support systems.
  • Robustness: failure-mode analysis of models deployed in real-world settings.
  • Fairness: how model behaviour differs across languages and cultures.
